How it works

Three steps. Zero servers.

1

Join a network

Connect to the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot. Internet is optional.

2

Peers appear

Lantern finds the other phones around you on the local network.

3

Just talk

Messages go straight from phone to phone. Nothing leaves the room.
